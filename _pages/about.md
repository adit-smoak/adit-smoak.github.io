I am a research associate at the [Indian Institute of Technology Bhubaneswar](https://www.iitbbs.ac.in/), working with Prof. Debapratim Ghosh on time-domain signal processing for antenna measurements.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **machine learning** and **reconfigurable computing**. I am particularly interested in how high-level synthesis can produce FPGA accelerators shaped by the models they run, starting with structured convolutional networks and extending to workloads with irregular computation, such as graph neural networks.

Previously, I was a project assistant in the Digital Audio Processing Lab at IIT Bombay with Prof. Preeti Rao, where I evaluated neural bandwidth-restoration models for archival music recordings. Before that, I was a research intern at the National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO), working on hyperspectral and satellite image analysis. I received my B.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, with Honours in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, from Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering, Mumbai, in 2026.

**I am currently applying to graduate programs for Fall 2027.**

Built as a team for the Smart India Hackathon 2024 (finals at IIT Roorkee), where we won first place and an award of ₹1,00,000 from the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

* Applied atmospheric correction with Py6S and a flat-field approach, to reduce the effect of light scattering and absorption by atmospheric gases.
* Denoised the hyperspectral cube by fine-tuning the QRNN3D model across multiple noise types.
* Implemented and trained a semi-supervised Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) for anomaly detection, working from a research paper whose code had not been released.
